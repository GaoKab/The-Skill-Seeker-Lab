# EWAH Collection Architecture

Catalogue of every style shown on the design boards, organized by capsule. Each entry records the design intent captured on the boards so nothing is lost between concept and factory.

---

## 01 — EWAH Sculpt
*Core modest-performance line for African and curvier bodies.*

### Sculpt Tunic
- Long-sleeve performance tunic in Aubergine
- Half-zip mock neck, thumbhole cuffs
- Asymmetric wrap-style hem covering hips and seat
- Sculpted seam lines through the torso
- Worn over Curve Legging or shorts

### Curve Legging
- High-rise compression legging, engineered for curvier proportions
- Contour seam lines (not just side seams) — waist-to-ankle sculpting
- No front rise seam camel-toe issue: gusset construction
- Squat-proof compression knit

### Flow Training Dress
- Sleeveless fitted bodice with double-tier flared skirt (peplum-over-skirt effect)
- Sculpted princess seams through the body
- Built-in shorts
- Aubergine signature colorway

### Purple Sculpt Tunic Dress
- Longer tunic silhouette designed to be worn **as a dress**
- Long sleeves, half-zip mock neck, thumbholes
- Curved shirttail hem (longer at back)
- Sculpted seam lines for shape and strength — modest yet powerful
- Gold EWAH branding: chest front + vertical spine at back
- Premium performance fabric

## 02 — EWAH Pleat Flow
*Pleated movement pieces with strength, elegance, and built-in performance.*

### Pleated Flow Dress
- Sleeveless fitted bodice, crew neck
- Knife-pleated skirt from dropped waist
- Built-in shorts
- Onyx

### Center Pleat Dress
- Sleeveless, smooth A-line skirt with a controlled center pleat detail
- Sculpted bodice seams
- Onyx

### Flow Pleat Skirt
- Standalone knife-pleat skirt, dropped yoke waistband
- Built-in shorts with pocket
- Gold emblem at hem

### Pleated Dress — Back Variation
- Signature pleated skirt meets an elevated back design
- Sculpted back paneling: contoured seam architecture across the upper back and racerback
- Vertical gold EWAH branding at upper back
- "Creates a strong, contoured back"

### Warrior Flow Dress ★ (hero product)
- Sleeveless, crew neck, fitted through the bodice
- Pleats concentrated at the back and sides; smooth front panel
- **Subtle side slit** for full range of motion and effortless flow
- **Built-in shorts** — discreet, secure, performance-ready, with **side phone pocket**
- Premium performance fabric: 4-way stretch, moisture-wicking, breathable, squat-proof
- Gold emblem near hem at slit; vertical EWAH spine branding at back
- Tagline: *Designed to move. Made to empower.*
