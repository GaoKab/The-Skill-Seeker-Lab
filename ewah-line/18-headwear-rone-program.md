## Brief A — EWAH Hat Block 01 / Sculpt Essential

**Product intent:** the clean architectural everyday foundation bucket hat, built from Gao's real wearing evidence — medium-depth crown, gently downward brim, quiet structure, real sun coverage, compatible with large sunglasses; never floppy, never oversized. The tan layered-band hat is the proportion reference.

| Field | Spec (starting; validate on head) |
|---|---|
| Block | Original pattern — NOT a modified stock blank. 5-panel or 6-panel crown study; brim with soft laminated interlining (no cardboard-stiff ring) |
| Crown | Medium depth ~10–11 cm; internal volume for braids, natural hair, low bun/ponytail, wig — fit sessions must cover all hair states + glasses |
| Brim | ~6.5–7.5 cm, gently downward; holds shape after packing (memory interlining) |
| Sizing | S/M + M/L with internal adjuster (hidden drawcord or gripper band — no external hardware) |
| Fabric | Outer: matte performance twill or the apparel platform-A knit bonded for structure; lining: wicking mesh or soft anti-slip band; Onyx first |
| Sweatband | **Hairline Contact Zone (core requirement, from founder's lived experience of hairline erosion):** two-layer system — hair → smooth low-friction contact facing (satin/silk-like performance face) → moisture-management structure beneath. Wide, soft pressure distribution (no narrow band concentrating on one line); no abrasive binding, exposed rough seams, or hard components at forehead/temples; adjuster hardware and bulky seams banned from the temple/hairline area | 
| Brooch interface | Reinforced attachment point at left side panel (see Brief B); on performance versions riveted/fixed, on lifestyle removable |
| Branding | The brooch IS the branding; at most a small tonal interior transfer. No printed logo on the shell |
| Tests | Wear (gym/walk/travel), wind (brim stability), sweat + wash, packability recovery, sunglasses clearance, **hair-friction protocol**: several hours' wear incl. training — does it migrate, does the band grab the edges, is there a pressure line on removal, is rotation abrasive |

**Factory questions:** Can you develop an original block from measurements rather than adapt a stock blank? What interlining options hold a soft-structured brim through packing? Can the crown be graded in two sizes with a hidden adjuster? MOQ for a 2-size, 1-color first run?

## Brief B — Emblem-Brooch hardware specification

**Intent:** the interlocked EWAH emblem (`13`) as a custom metal trim with a technical purpose — anchoring drapes, wrap panels, and gathers — not a promotional pin.

| Field | Spec |
|---|---|
| Artwork | Emblem vector from the identity designer (same blocking file flagged in `13`) — hardware supplier needs DXF/STEP or clean vector |
| Size | 22–28 mm working range; test at 22 / 25 / 28 against the actual crown |
| Material | Zinc alloy or brass; plating: brushed antique gold (primary — match #B28D4F family), blackened gunmetal, antique pewter |
| Edges | All edges rounded, snag-tested against knit and mesh swatches |
| Attachment | Two systems to prototype: (1) locking pin-back with safety rotate-lock for removable use; (2) rivet/screw-post for fixed performance use. No simple butterfly clutch (loses, snags, injures) |
| Integrity | Pull test ≥ 70 N on fixed version; corrosion: salt-spray test (sweat!) 24 h minimum; nickel-safe plating for skin contact |
| Weight | Premium-feel target 8–14 g at 25 mm — heavy enough to feel real, light enough not to distort knit |
| Packaging | Individually carded for removable versions (doubles as an accessory SKU later) |

**Supplier route:** custom metal trim/badge makers (separate RFQ from the sewing factory — hardware is its own industry). Sample tooling for a custom emblem typically carries a one-time mold fee (~$100–300) plus low per-unit cost; get the mold fee quoted upfront and **EWAH owns the tooling** — put it in writing.

## Brief C — RONE Reverence Short Drape

**Intent:** one integrated modular piece — a pre-sculpted crown/head wrap with an attached SHORT shoulder drape — for funerals and memorials in Botswana. It must complement the wearer's dress, never overpower it (the long cape version is rejected). Matte black, restrained, dignified, no visible athletic detailing.

| Field | Spec (starting) |
|---|---|
| Construction | Single garment: sculpted crown (darted/paneled, pre-shaped — no user wrapping skill needed) + attached drape falling to cover shoulders |
| Drape length | Short: covering shoulder point to upper arm, ~28–35 cm from crown edge — validate on multiple wearers; must not read as a cape |
| Fit | Internal anti-slip band + adjustable closure; interior volume for braids, wigs, natural hair |
| Comfort | Hidden ventilation (Botswana heat), soft lining, breathable matte fabric with weight enough to drape still (crepe-jersey family, platform D) |
| Hardware | Removable brooch: blackened gunmetal / pewter / dark antique gold — or none for strict funeral use |
| Variants (later) | RONE Sunday (restrained colors), RONE Heritage (leteisi-inspired fabric, group ordering) — same block |
| Cultural validation | Before production: the Botswana research step below is a **gate**, not a nice-to-have |
| Naming | "RONE" (from *korone*/crown) is PROVISIONAL — check trademark availability in Botswana + key markets, pronunciation, and meaning-collision before printing anything |

**Botswana research guide (run before Reverence sampling):** 8–12 conversations with women across funeral, church, and traditional-ceremony contexts: What do you wear on your head now, and who decides? What fails (slipping, heat, re-wrapping, transport)? Would one pre-shaped piece be welcomed or seen as a shortcut? Appropriate drape length by context? Group/uniform ordering behavior (church societies, burial societies — likely the real wholesale channel)? Price anchors vs. current solutions. Record verbatim, not summarized.

## RFQ split

Two separate supplier tracks — do not ask a garment factory for metal hardware or vice versa:
1. **Headwear cut-and-sew** (custom hat blocks, soft-structured brims): RFQ carries Briefs A + C, watermarked boards, custom-measurement instruction, same escrow/vetting rules as `10`–`11`
2. **Metal trim/hardware maker:** RFQ carries Brief B + emblem vector; mold ownership clause

## Program sequencing vs. Capsule 01

The headwear program is **parallel and lower-cost** (hat sampling typically $30–80/piece; brooch mold ~$100–300) but it must not consume the Capsule 01 Round-1 budget. Order of cash: **WF-01 + ST-02 apparel samples first**, then Sculpt Essential + brooch as the second spend, then Reverence after the Botswana research gate. Scorecards and revision logs for hats reuse the `08` §10 / master-pack format.
