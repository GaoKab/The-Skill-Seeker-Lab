## The production reality — this is a DECORATION project, not cut-and-sew

The board specifies **Comfort Colors 1717**, a stocked US blank. That single line decides everything:

| Item | Right maker | Wrong maker |
|---|---|---|
| 5 tees + cropped tee + hoodie | **Screen printer / decorator** (US, holds Comfort Colors in stock) | A Chinese cut-and-sew factory |
| Beanie + bucket hat | **Custom hat factory (Aung Crown fits)** | A screen printer |

**This is the opposite of EWAH.** EWAH's value is the pattern block, so decoration-on-blanks was rejected there. PULA's value is the *artwork and the story* — so blanks + excellent decoration is exactly right, and it is fast, cheap, and low-risk.

**Recommendation — split into two drops, and let the split be part of the story:**
1. **BOIPUSO 60 drop, 30 September:** tees + the fast celebration objects (can cooler, coasters). Two or three tee designs, not five. Order this week or not at all.
2. **RAIN OBJECTS drop, rain season (Nov–March):** beanie, bucket hat, jacket, pin, keyring — properly sampled. Botswana's rain falls Nov–March, so *rain objects released for the rain* is better storytelling than a rushed September delivery, and it gives the reflective and cord details the development time they need.

**The gating item is not a supplier — it is the vector artwork.** Tech pack v1 forbids printing from the mockups. Until clean vector separations exist, no printer can quote or proof. That is the critical path for the 30 September drop.

## Print-technique notes for whoever quotes it
- **3D puff** on the big PULA letterforms — puff needs plastisol; ask the printer to show a puff sample before running
- **Rain lines** in fine HD detail + optional **3M reflective ink** — reflective is a specialty add; confirm the printer stocks it and quote with/without
- Fine text (coordinates) — minimum readable stroke; ask for a press proof
- Garment-dyed blanks vary slightly in shade batch to batch — normal for Comfort Colors, mention it in product copy rather than fighting it

## IP and cultural checks before selling
- **"PULA" and "A ene!!" cannot and should not be trademarked** — Pula is Botswana's national motto and currency; A ene is common Setswana. Own the *brand mark and artwork*, never the words themselves. This is a feature: the collection honours a shared thing rather than claiming it.
- **Flag usage:** the side tab uses Botswana flag colours. Check Botswana's national symbols rules for commercial use of the flag before printing at scale; a stylised colour bar is usually safer than an exact flag reproduction.
- Register the **artwork** (the rain-line system, droplet mark, PULA lockup) as your copyright — that IS protectable and is the real asset.
- **FATSHE LENO** is the opening of the national anthem (*Fatshe leno la rona*) — a strong, respectful choice for a 60th anniversary piece, and non-exclusive by nature.
- **Check "PEOPLE / LAND / OPPORTUNITY" before ever reviving it.** Tech pack v1 already bans it; verify whether it carries a party-political association in Botswana. A commemorative collection must stay national, never partisan.
- Keep PULA's supplier conversations, files, and any agreement in a separate thread from EWAH's, as already done.
