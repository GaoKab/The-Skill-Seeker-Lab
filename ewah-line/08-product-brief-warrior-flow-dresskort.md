## 1. Customer problem

A curvier woman training seriously has no single garment that gives her, at once: real coverage of stomach, hips and seat; a shape that celebrates her waist without compressing it; performance-fabric function; and beauty she'd be proud to be seen in. Her current options force a choice — leggings-and-top combinations that expose and cling, or modest layers that are shapeless, hot, and visually apologetic. The Dress-Skort is one garment that ends that choice: sculpted through the body, flowing over the hips, shorts secured underneath, engineered for actual training.

It must solve, in one piece, fit failures **2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8** of the framework catalogue (bust strain, cling, transparency, ride-up, apologetic plus styling, hidden-not-powerful), and its liner shorts must solve **1, 4, 9** (waist gap, band roll — with **no front rise seam**, Design Standard rule 9).

## 2. Intended activities

**Engineered for:** strength training (squats, deadlifts, lunges, presses, machine and bench work), functional/cross training, gym floor work, warmups, fast walking, studio classes.
**Compatible:** light running, everyday wear (gym-to-street).
**Not the design target:** long-distance running, swimming, contact sports.
It is a *training garment*, tested like equipment — not gym-themed leisurewear.

## 3. Silhouette

**Front:** Fitted sculpted bodice, sleeveless, modest crew/high-crew neckline. From the natural waist, an **asymmetrical wrap front**: a smooth panel crosses the body diagonally (high hip on wrap side → lower opposite hem), reading as a wrap skirt. Skirt volume is controlled — flares with movement, falls clean at rest. Front hem sits mid-thigh with the liner never exposed while standing.

**Back:** Continuous sculpted seam architecture from shoulder through waist. Skirt back is the **pleated zone** — knife pleats from a smooth dropped yoke, giving stride room and movement flow. Vertical gold EWAH branding down the upper spine.

**Side (required view in flats):** the wrap overlay's leading edge and the pleat-to-smooth transition both live at the sides — the side view is where this garment's construction is actually explained. Wrap-side overlap must remain secure (no gaping) at full stride and squat depth.

## 4. Sculpted seam map

- **Bodice front:** two curved princess lines from armhole through bust apex zone to waist — these deliver the bust allowance as *shaping*, not stretch strain (fit rule: dart-equivalent in the seam, Failure 2)
- **Bodice back:** curved back panels converging to the waist; seams continue visually into the skirt yoke — one uninterrupted line language
- **Waist:** seamed at natural waist; shaping defines the waist **without excessive compression** (Design Standard rule 2) — silhouette from geometry, not elastic squeeze
- **Skirt:** wrap overlay edge is a clean finished curve (faced, not merely hemmed); no horizontal seams across the seat
- All sculpting seams flatlocked in self-color; seam lines are the design — no topstitch contrast

## 5. Pleated vs. smooth panel map

| Zone | Treatment | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Front wrap overlay | **Smooth** | Clean line over the stomach; no added volume at front fullness (FB-2) |
| Front under-layer (wrap side) | Smooth | Overlay glides over it |
| Side transition panels | Smooth → pleat lead-in | Controlled hand-off; pleats must not start at the widest hip point |
| Back skirt | **Knife pleats** from dropped yoke | Movement flow + stride room over glutes without gripping (FB-1) |
| Back yoke (waist to high hip) | Smooth | Anchors pleats below the seat's widest curve; keeps volume controlled |

Pleats selective, not everywhere (rule 6): pleated area ≤ ~40% of skirt circumference. Pleats heat-set permanent (fabric platform B), depth ~2.5 cm, closed at yoke seam.

## 6. Built-in short requirements

- Attached liner short, **no front rise seam** (rule 9) — front cut on fold; diamond gusset; flat coverstitched inseams
- Inseam **13 cm (5")** base; validate 4"–6" preference with the testing circle
- Waist: integrated into the dress waist seam with its own soft internal band — the *liner* carries the anti-gap engineering (contoured, higher back rise for FB-1; no roll under front fullness for FB-2)
- **Right-side drop-in phone pocket**, fits 6.7" phone, opening angled forward, bounce-tested; optional small key loop
- Hem: clean coverstitched fold — **no silicone gripper** (chafe + hygiene); anti-ride achieved by leg shaping and correct circumference at the thigh
- Liner never visible standing; at squat depth the skirt may rise but the liner reads as intentional coverage, not underwear
- Liner opacity squat-proof in every colorway independently of the skirt
- **Open question for validation (testing-circle Q7):** removable vs. sewn-in liner. Proto 1 is sewn-in; if testers demand removable, Proto 2 explores a buttoned/looped detachable liner — decision by data, not assumption

## 7. Fabric requirements

| Component | Platform (from `03-fabrics-and-materials.md`) | Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| Bodice + smooth skirt panels | **A** — recycled nylon-elastane compression knit, 200–220 gsm | Matte, non-clinging hand (rule 4); 4-way stretch; squat-proof opacity wet and dry; sweat-wicking |
| Pleated back zone | **B** — poly warp knit, 160–190 gsm | Permanent heat-set pleat retention ≥ 5 washes; color-matched to platform A in Onyx |
| Liner short | **A** at 220–240 gsm | Full compression-zone opacity; wicking; cool touch |
| Facings/binding | Self-fabric A | Neckline + armhole clean bound, no elastic edges visible |

Launch colorway: **Onyx**. Cross-platform color match (A vs. B in the same garment) approved via lab dips in daylight before proto 2.

## 8. Fit specifications

Base development size M, cut on the EWAH Curve Block. Values below are the design targets to be proven in Proto 1; each is then validated per fit body (FB-1 / FB-2 / FB-3) and encoded into grading.

| Dimension | Specification |
|---|---|
| Bust allowance | +4–6 cm ease over full bust delivered via princess shaping; zero strain lines at strap/apex (FB-3 check) |
| Waist-to-hip difference | Block cut at W:H 0.72; skirt release at waist seam accommodates to 0.68 without pull |
| Front / back rise (liner) | Front 26 cm / back 30 cm (size M) — back rise +4 cm differential for FB-1 |
| Garment length | HPS–front hem 88 cm; wrap low point +5 cm; back hem 92 cm (covers seat + pleat fall) |
| Rear coverage | Standing: hem ≥ 12 cm below gluteal fold. Squat depth: hem stays below the fold — verified in movement, not on the stand |
| Armhole depth | Deep enough for overhead press without side-bra exposure; bound edge sits flush at full reach |
| Sleeve circumference | n/a (sleeveless) — armhole opening ease serves the fuller upper arm; no cut-in shoulder |
| Built-in short inseam | 13 cm; thigh opening sized to clear (not grip) the fuller thigh |
| Movement allowance | Squat: +25% circumferential stretch budget at seat without opacity loss. Hinge: back hem drop coverage maintained. Overhead: front hem rise ≤ 5 cm, self-recovering. Stride: pleat zone opens; wrap stays closed |

## 10. Testing criteria (pass/fail for prototype approval)

Movement circuit (on FB-representative wearers, real training session):

- [ ] 10 deep squats — liner opaque, hem below gluteal fold at depth, waist anchored (≤ 2 cm migration), wrap closed
- [ ] Deadlifts — back waistband no gape > 1 cm; pleats fall back clean after each rep
- [ ] Lunges (walking, deep) — wrap overlay stays closed at full stride; no thigh exposure above liner
- [ ] Overhead press — front hem rise ≤ 5 cm, self-recovers, no tug needed
- [ ] Smith machine + bench — pleats do **not** snag on pads, rails, or velcro; garment doesn't pool or trap on the bench
- [ ] Floor work (plank, bridge, mat rolls) — nothing rides, twists, or exposes; pocket holds phone in all positions
- [ ] Fast walking + sitting — pleats recover from sitting creasing; skirt doesn't wing up in stride
- [ ] **Tug count over 45-minute session: 0**

Durability circuit:

- [ ] 5 home wash cycles — pleats hold, gold branding intact, no color shift between platforms A and B
- [ ] Soaked-sweat opacity test — no transparency at seat/bust when wet
- [ ] Pocket security — phone through 20 burpees
- [ ] Snag test — deliberate contact pass against gym velcro, rough bench edge, Smith rail

Any failed box = pattern/fabric correction and re-test. No "good enough for photos."

## 11. Prototype questions — what Proto 1 must answer

1. Does the asymmetric wrap stay closed through squat depth and full stride — or does it need an internal anchor (snap/stitch-down point)?
2. Do back pleats add power to the silhouette over prominent glutes — or add bulk at the widest point? (Determines pleat start height and count)
3. Is the smooth front overlay genuinely flattering over front-waist fullness (FB-2), or does it need more structured float?
4. Does one waist-seam construction serve both FB-1 (small waist, full hip) and FB-2 (full front waist) — or does this garment force the first block split?
5. Is 13 cm the right liner inseam across thigh fullness, or does chafe/coverage demand 15 cm?
6. Phone in the liner pocket during pleat movement — secure and invisible, or does it print through the skirt?
7. Can platforms A and B be color-matched in Onyx to production standard — or does the pleat zone read as a different black?
8. Where exactly must the pleat-to-smooth transition sit relative to the hip's widest point for each fit body?
9. Does the garment pass the snag test around machines, or do pleat depth/openness need reduction?
10. What does the wearer *instinctively tug* — the honest map of remaining fit failures?
