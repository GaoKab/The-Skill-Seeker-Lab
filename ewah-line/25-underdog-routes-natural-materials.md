# Underdog Manufacturing Routes + Natural Materials Strategy (researched Sept 2026)

Founder direction: explore under-estimated manufacturing countries (Portugal, Morocco, others) and manufacturers working with natural ("life") materials instead of plastic.

## The honest materials physics (read first)

**A squat-proof 4-way-stretch compression garment without synthetics does not currently exist** — stretch recovery IS elastane. The strategy is therefore a split, not a ban:

| Product family | Materials route | Plastic-free? |
|---|---|---|
| Bucket hats (shells) | Organic cotton / hemp twill shell, cotton lining — genuinely achievable now | ✅ (interlining + sweatband need checking; natural options exist) |
| Ease tee dresses, Throw-On layers | GOTS organic cotton, TENCEL lyocell, hemp blends | ✅ or near |
| RONE (crepe drape) | TENCEL/modal crepe blends | Mostly ✅ |
| Performance dresses / leggings / bras (Curve Block core) | Majority-natural possible (e.g., TENCEL or cotton body + elastane %) or recycled synthetics (GRS); biodegradable-elastane options (e.g., Roica V550 class) worth asking mills about | ❌ fully, ✅ majority — honesty in marketing required |

**Marketing rule:** never claim "plastic-free" on a garment containing elastane. "Majority plant-based," "recycled," or "plastic-free" only where literally true. Greenwashing would poison the brand's honesty positioning.

## Portugal — the double win (underdog route + naturals capital)

Portuguese mills are Europe's GOTS-organic heartland; small-brand friendliness is real. Candidates:

| Candidate | Reported terms | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **ASBX** (Porto) | MOQ ~50–100/style | Jersey-based garments incl. activewear-style; startup-oriented |
| **Athleisure Basics / ABM** (Porto) | MOQ ~100/style/color | Custom + white-label activewear specialist |
| **TMR Fashion Clothing** | MOQ 25 (B&W) / 50 (colors) | Very low MOQ |
| **Friendly Factories** | MOQ 20–200 | Multi-product, high startup-friendliness |

Trade-offs: 2–4× China unit cost · EU IP enforceability (strongest trust profile, per `14`) · easy factory visits · natural/GOTS fabric sourcing on the doorstep.

## Morocco — promising but thin at small scale

Real knit/garment industry (Casablanca, Tangier; EU-oriented), duty-free to EU, close to Iberia. BUT: low-MOQ, startup-facing, English-speaking front doors are scarce — most capacity serves large EU retail contracts. Route in: the **Maroc in Mode** trade show / AMITH (industry association) directory rather than cold email. Verdict: keep on the map for production scale-up (Phase 2+), not for Round-1 sampling.
