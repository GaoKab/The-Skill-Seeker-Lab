# EWAH Brand Identity Specification

Source of truth: `boards/brand-identity-sheet.png`. This document translates the identity into production-usable specs for factories, label suppliers, and packaging.

**Positioning line:** Luxury activewear for African & curvier bodies.

## Marks

| Mark | Description | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary wordmark** | E W A H — Cormorant Garamond, wide letter-spacing, sculpted serif details | Web headers, hang tags, packaging, chest branding (small) |
| **Signature emblem** | Interlocked E-W-A-H monogram inside a gold oval — sculptural, movement in balance | Woven labels, back-neck emblem, zip pulls, hem marks, app icon |
| **Vertical back-lockup** | Emblem above E-W-A-H stacked vertically + "Sculpt · Flow · Freedom" | The signature spine branding at center back of garments |

**Letter meanings** (brand storytelling, hang-tag copy): **E** Strength/Structure · **W** Flow/Movement · **A** Form/Architecture · **H** Harmony/Balance.

**Tagline system:** *Sculpt · Flow · Freedom* is the identity tagline (labels, tags, lockups). *Sculpted, not exposed.* remains the positioning line (marketing copy, campaign). *Your body was never the sizing problem. The clothes were.* remains the emotional line.

## Color (official, from the identity sheet)

| Color | Hex | Production reference |
|---|---|---|
| Onyx | `#0E0E0E` | PANTONE Black 6 C |
| Warm Ivory | `#F6F2EC` | ≈ PANTONE 9224 C (supersedes "Bone" naming — same family) |
| Antique Gold | `#B28D4F` | ≈ PANTONE 871–872 C metallic / 4515 C matte print — **lab-dip/thread-match against #B28D4F**, approve in daylight |

Garment palette (Onyx, Clay, Rust, Aubergine, Olive + Warm Ivory) per `03` remains for fabrics; the three identity colors govern branding, labels, tags, and packaging.

## Typography

**Cormorant Garamond** (Google Fonts, open license — free for commercial use). Wordmark and headings; generous tracking. Pair with a clean geometric sans (e.g. Montserrat/Inter, light weights, wide tracking) for technical/body text as seen on the boards.

## Garment application (updates the branding standard in `02`)

| Placement | Mark | Spec |
|---|---|---|
| Center back, upper spine | Vertical back-lockup (emblem + stacked letters) | ~11 × 2.5 cm; Antique Gold matte heat transfer (performance knits) or gold-thread embroidery (heavy knits) |
| Chest front / hem | Emblem only | ≤ 2 cm; minimal front branding rule holds |
| Main label (back neck) | Black woven label: emblem + EWAH + "SCULPT · FLOW · FREEDOM" | Damask weave, Onyx ground, Antique Gold thread — per mockup |
| Hang tag | Black card, gold emblem + EWAH + tagline stacked | Recycled matte black card, gold foil or metallic ink, brass-tone eyelet, black cord |
| Zip pulls | Emblem debossed | Antique-gold-tone hardware |

## Production checklist (what the factory/label supplier needs)

- [ ] **Vector files** of wordmark, emblem, and vertical lockup (AI/EPS/PDF + SVG) — request from the identity designer; PNG mockups cannot be embroidered or woven. This is the single blocking item
- [ ] Emblem minimum sizes: test weave/embroidery legibility at 15 mm (interlocked strokes may need a simplified small-size variant — ask the label maker)
- [ ] Thread/foil match to `#B28D4F` approved on physical samples in daylight
- [ ] Woven label + hang tag suppliers: order with the sample round so approved samples arrive fully branded (label MOQs are low — typically 100–500 pcs, ~$50–150)
- [ ] Trademark filing should cover **both** the EWAH wordmark and the emblem device (per `07` §8)

## Usage rules

1. Gold marks on Onyx or dark grounds; Onyx marks on Warm Ivory grounds — never gold on light-mid tones where it vanishes
2. Front of garment stays near-silent; the back spine carries the identity (matches the product design codes)
3. Emblem never stretched, recolored outside the three identity colors, or locked up with other graphics
4. Tagline always tracked-out capitals with interpuncts: SCULPT · FLOW · FREEDOM
