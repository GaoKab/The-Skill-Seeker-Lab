Hey [name],

I'm building something new and I immediately thought of you. I've founded a performance-wear brand called **EWAH** — luxury movement wear engineered around African and curvier bodies — and the first physical product isn't a garment: it's a piece of metal.

We're developing a **signature emblem medallion** — think of the role Hermès' H hardware plays — that will appear across the whole brand: hats, ceremonial wraps, and eventually garments and accessories. I have the emblem as flat vector artwork and a full engineering spec (dimensions, profile, materials, finishes). What I need is exactly the thing you're brilliant at: **translating flat artwork into jewelry-grade 3D geometry** that a die-striking manufacturer can tool from.

**The scope (small and finite):**

1. **3D CAD of the medallion** — 25 mm diameter, lens/dome profile (~3 mm center to ~2 mm rounded rim), the interlocked EWAH emblem in raised relief on a solid face, flat back with space for a fine engraved line. I'll send the full spec sheet — stroke depths and draft angles need to suit die-striking in brass.
2. **One physical check** — a resin print or single cast so we can hold it and judge the relief before tooling money is spent.
3. **A finishing consult** — your eye on how the relief will behave in brushed antique gold PVD and matte black.

Deliverables: the editable source CAD + manufacturing-ready STEP/STL, the print/cast, **high-quality renders in all three finishes** (antique gold, gunmetal, matte black), your finishing notes, and **a short written note on why you chose the proportions and relief depths** — I archive design reasoning so future versions can build on it.

(You won't be discovering the object from scratch — I'll send a decided design sheet with the spec pack: outline shape, relief levels, rim width, finish contrast, engraving placement, edge profile, and size. Your craft goes into executing it beautifully, and pushing back where you think a decision is wrong.)

**On fee — two options, your pick:**
- **A flat project fee** you quote for the scope above (I'm a bootstrapping founder, so I'll be honest about budget, but I want this done properly and paid fairly), or
- **A smaller flat fee + a royalty** on the emblem when it launches as a standalone accessory — the medallion is designed to become a purchasable piece in its own right, so this could actually be worth something.

And separately from this commission: down the line I'd love to talk about a **named collaboration** — a fine-jewelry openwork edition of the emblem as "EWAH × 89 Carat Street." That's a future conversation once the brand has an audience; I mention it so you know how I see this relationship going.

One piece of housekeeping I do with everyone (friends especially, so nothing ever gets weird): a short agreement that the CAD files and anything derived from them (tooling, molds) are EWAH's property as work-for-hire, kept confidential until launch — and you keep portfolio credit rights by permission, which I'll happily give. Terms below; if they look fine, reply "agreed" and we're off.

Would you be up for it? I can send the spec pack the same day you say yes.

[Your name]
