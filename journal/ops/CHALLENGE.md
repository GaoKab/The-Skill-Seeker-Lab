# The Skill Seeker Challenge — Season 1
*Gao's original intent, stated 25 Sep: Skill Seeker was always a **challenge** — she learns one skill in public, shares what she's learning, people follow along or join, and **YouTube is the main driver.** The journal is the participation kit. This document turns that into a runnable season. It is a skeleton until the four questions at the bottom are answered.*

## The shape
- **One season = one skill = one journal = twelve weeks.** Gao does the journal on camera. Anyone can join by buying the journal and starting the same Monday.
- **The journal is the show's format.** Week Brief = the Monday episode open. Daily Lab = the shorts. Week Debrief = the episode close. **The Friction Log is the content engine** — "where I got stuck today" is the most watchable sentence in learning.
- **The email list is the cohort.** Monday: the week's brief and the rep. Nothing else all week. The `bought-journal` tag is the cohort; week 10's email asks *what's the next skill?* — which is the next season, and the next journal.

## Cadence (lean, phone-filmed, sustainable next to a job search)
| Where | What | How often | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **YouTube** | Week Debrief episode: what I tried, where it broke, what I keep. 8–12 min, one take, phone on a stand, window light. | 1/wk (Sunday) | 1 h film + 1 h cut |
| **YouTube Shorts / IG Reels** | The Friction Log line of the day, read to camera, 20–40 s | 3/wk | 10 min each |
| **Instagram feed** | Week Brief card (Mon) · "join this week" · one Evidence Ledger photo (Fri) | 3/wk | prepared by GM |
| **Email** | Monday brief to the cohort | 1/wk | drafted by GM |
| **Community** | one hashtag; reshare joiners' friction logs | ongoing | 15 min/day |

**Season 1 rule:** Gao is a participant, not a presenter. She does the journal for real; the camera watches. If the skill isn't genuinely hers, the season is content, and it will feel like it.

## Metrics that matter (not views)
- **Joiners:** journals sold in the 2 announce weeks + week 1 (KDP) · emails on the `bought-journal` tag
- **Retention:** cohort still posting at week 6 and week 12
- **Watch time** on Week Debriefs (YouTube's own signal) over subscriber count
- **Season 2 conversion:** share of Season 1 joiners who buy the next journal — the whole model in one number

## Risks
- **Bandwidth.** Twelve weeks is long. The cadence above is the minimum that still counts as a season; nothing gets added until week 4 is on the board.
- **Public failure.** A skill that stalls at week 7 is the *best* possible episode — the Friction Log exists for it. Say so in the trailer.
- **Two brands, one person.** Skill Seeker is Gao on camera. Before the Sermon is a voice without a face. Keep the accounts separate; cross-mention once a season at most.
