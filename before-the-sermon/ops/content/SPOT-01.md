**Design rule applied to every card:** the odd one out contradicts a plain fact stated inside the cited verses — a number, a named object, a stated order of events — never a matter of timing, emphasis or inference. That's what makes it unarguable.

## 01 · Luke 10:30–35 — the man on the road to Jericho
A) A priest saw the man and passed by on the other side
B) A Levite came to the place, looked, and also passed by
C) The Samaritan bandaged his wounds, pouring on oil and wine
D) The Samaritan paid the innkeeper three denarii

**Answer: D.** Verse 35 — *two* denarii. (A: v31. B: v32. C: v34.)
**Caption:** Most people get this one wrong, and it isn't a trick. It's a number you've heard read aloud a hundred times and never once needed. That's the point of reading it yourself: you find out which parts you've been letting someone else carry.

## 02 · Mark 4:35–41 — the storm
A) Jesus was asleep in the stern, on a cushion
B) The disciples asked whether he cared that they were about to die
C) Jesus spoke to the sea itself: "Peace. Be still."
D) Once the wind dropped, the disciples' fear left them

**Answer: D.** Verse 41 — after the calm, they were *more* afraid, not less ("they feared exceedingly"). (A: v38. B: v38. C: v39.)
**Caption:** The wind stops. The sea goes flat. And the text says their fear got bigger. Read that verse again before Sunday and notice whether the sermon mentions it.

## 03 · Genesis 1:1–5 — the first day
A) God said, "Let there be light," and there was light
B) God saw that the light was good
C) God called the light Day and the darkness Night
D) God made the sun on the first day

**Answer: D.** The sun and moon are made on the fourth day (1:16). Days 1–5 have light, but no sun. (A: v3. B: v4. C: v5.)
**Caption:** Light on day one. The sun on day four. Whatever you make of that — and you don't have to make anything of it yet — you have to see it first.

## 04 · Ruth 1:16–17 — what Ruth said
A) Where you go, I will go
B) Your people will be my people, and your God my God
C) Where you die, I will die, and there I will be buried
D) Ruth promised to return to Moab if Naomi died first

**Answer: D.** Verse 17 — Ruth binds herself with an oath that nothing but death will part them; there is no return to Moab anywhere in the two verses. (A: v16. B: v16. C: v17.)
**Caption:** Everyone knows the first line. Almost nobody knows the last one, and it's the one that changes the speech from affection into a vow.
