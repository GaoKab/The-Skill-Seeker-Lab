# 08 · Risk and Quality
*Standing rules. The GM enforces them; Gao can override any of them in writing.*

**Copyright and Scripture.** Default: cite the reference, don't reprint the verse. If quoting: ≤2 verses, public-domain translation (WEB, KJV) *or* Gao's own translation with the publisher's required attribution line (ESV, NIV, CSB all permit short quotation without permission). Never reproduce a study-Bible note, a commentary, or another creator's reading. Policy decision: D-07.

**Attribution.** Every idea we borrow gets named. Nothing from She Reads Truth, The Bible Recap, or any creator is paraphrased as ours.

**Factual accuracy.** No claims about what a passage "means" — that would violate the product itself. No statistics without a source in the post file. No "studies show".

**Platform compliance.** Amazon: never incentivise reviews; no review swaps; author may ask real buyers for *honest* reviews. IG: no engagement bait; religious content is permitted — keep it non-political and non-denominational, which is also the brand.

**Brand safety.** Non-denominational by design. No commentary on churches, pastors, or public figures. No "your pastor is wrong" energy — the name is *before*, not *against*, the sermon.

**AI-generated material.** Images: mood only, ≤3 weeks, never captioned as the product, no synthetic people or hands (D-10). Text: GM drafts; Gao writes or approves every published caption; anything that sounds like it could be on any account gets cut. The tell for generic AI copy in this niche: stacked adjectives, "journey", "embrace", "unlock". Banned.

**Reputational.** The founder's other brand (DustOff Reset) and job-search posts stay on their own accounts. Before the Sermon's account speaks only in the notebook's voice.

**Money.** $0 spend for 90 days except author proofs (~$8 + shipping) and, if approved, a domain. Every spend is a decision in `05-DECISIONS.md`.

**Data.** Email subscribers are told what they'll get (one Sunday email) and can leave in one click. No purchased lists, ever.
