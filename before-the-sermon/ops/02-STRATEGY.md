# 02 · Strategy
*Proposed by the GM · requires Gao's approval (see `05-DECISIONS.md` D-01…D-04)*

## Core promise
**Sit with the text before anyone explains it.**
A structured, unintimidating way to read Scripture for yourself — before the sermon, the summary, or the commentary — and to come away with your own reading, not someone else's.

## Primary audience
**The intimidated reader.** Someone who owns a Bible, wants to read it, and quietly doesn't — because they don't know how, they're afraid of getting it wrong, or every resource they've tried tells them what it means before they've had a chance to look. Includes church-goers who only ever meet Scripture *through* a sermon, and cautious seekers who won't sit through a devotional's assumptions.

Secondary: **the slow-reading Christian** who is tired of plans and wants depth; **small-group leaders** who need a tool that doesn't pre-empt the discussion.

Explicitly not chasing: people who want a reading plan, a devotional, or a theology explainer. The market is full of those and they are cheap.

## The problem / desire
*"I don't know how to read this on my own."* Underneath it: the fear of being wrong, and the fatigue of being told. The desire is confidence — not certainty about meaning, but confidence as a reader.

## Unique point of view
1. **Reading comes before interpretation, and most people have never been allowed to do the first without the second.**
2. **Confusion is a valid outcome.** *I don't know* is a complete answer. No one else in the category says this.
3. **We never tell you what it means.** Every competitor's value is explanation. Ours is the withholding of it.
4. **No belief assumed.** The door is open to the curious and the cautious.

## Competitive frame
| Player | What they sell | Where we differ |
|---|---|---|
| She Reads Truth (919K IG) | Curated plans + devotional explanation, beautiful print | They explain. We don't. |
| The Bible Recap | Daily recap that summarises the text | Summary *is* the sermon. We're the moment before it. |
| Dwell | Audio listening habit | Passive intake. We're active, on paper. |
| Amazon "Bible study journal" shelf | $6–20 spiral/all-in-one kits, tabs, stickers; top seller sells ~5,000/mo at $40 | Volume, feature-stuffed, assumes belief. We're one method, one object, considered. |

The white space: **pre-interpretation, for the unconfident, without assumption.** Small compared with SRT's audience, but nobody is standing in it.

## Repeatable formats
| Format | Cadence | Shape | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sit With It carousel** | 1/wk | Slide 1 passage ref + "read it twice". Slides 2–4 one question each with white space. Slide 5 "what did you notice? — no right answers". | Save-driven; the product demonstrated. |
| **One Question card** | 2/wk | Single cream text card, one of the six questions, one line of hint. | Cheap, on-brand, builds recognition of the six. |
| **Before Sunday** | Sat, weekly | Passage for tomorrow + one question. | Ritual; the name; shareable to church friends. |
| **Quiet Reel** | 1/wk | 12–20 s, no voice, pages turning / writing, one caption line. | IG reach without performing. |
| **Story: "What confused you this week?"** | Sun/Mon | Question sticker; reshare answers (with permission). | Community; UGC in the voice. |
| **Email: The Sunday Page** | Weekly | One passage, the six questions, printable. | List-building; the free spread is the lead magnet. |

## Growth model
Organic IG (saves + shares over likes) → link to a **free printable spread** (email capture) → weekly Sunday email → Amazon. Collaborations with small-group leaders and 5–30K faith accounts who share the "slow" ethos, not the megabrands. Pinterest as a controlled experiment in month 3 (journals index well there). Amazon listing tuned for "bible study journal for beginners" / "scripture reading notebook". **No paid media for 90 days.**

## Monetization model (in order)
1. **KDP paperback ($14.99) and hardcover ($24.99)** — live first, zero capital.
2. **Small-group / church bulk** (10+) — direct, once the proof copy exists.
3. **Linen Edition** ($29.99–34.99, offshore run of 500) — *only* after the gate in `../PRINT-ROUTES.md` is met.
4. **Companion PDFs** (the 52-passage reading list; a printable spread pack) — low-price digital, later.
5. **No ads, no sponsorships, no affiliate links** in the first year. The brand's asset is that it doesn't sell you anything mid-reading.

## What success looks like at day 90
Provisional (no baseline yet — recalibrate at week 4): notebook live in both formats; first 25 honest reviews; 150 paperbacks + 60 hardcovers sold; 300 email subscribers; IG cadence held for 12 straight weeks with saves/post trending up; a go/no-go decision on the linen run made on evidence.
