## What is strong

1. **The idea is sharp and defensible.** "Sit with the text before anyone explains it" is a real gap. Every major player — She Reads Truth (919K IG), The Bible Recap, Dwell — *explains*. Nobody owns the moment before explanation. This is a position, not just a product.
2. **The voice is already defined and unusually disciplined.** Calm/not authoritative, invitational/not instructional, reflective/not prescriptive, gentle/but serious. "Confusion, silence and *I don't know* are valid outcomes." That is a brand, and it is rare in this category.
3. **The audience includes people the category ignores** — the intimidated, the cautious seeker, the non-Christian who is curious. Competitors assume belief. This one says "no prior knowledge, belief, or background is assumed."
4. **Design intent is coherent** — cream, terracotta, Cormorant/Crimson/Lato, restraint. The mockups and site agree with each other.
5. **The product is now real.** Two days ago this was a concept in a JS bundle. It is now three upload-ready files.

## What is unclear or weak

1. **The six questions exist in two versions** (mockup vs site). The notebook currently uses a merged set I chose. Gao has not approved it. *This is the most important unapproved decision in the business.*
2. **Two cover lockups.** The notebook uses the all-caps serif. The script version is unresolved.
3. **The mockup object can't be made by Amazon** (linen, foil, ribbon). Route decided in `../PRINT-ROUTES.md`: KDP first, linen later if demand proves. Gao agreed in principle; formal sign-off pending.
4. **No proof of demand yet.** Zero sales, zero list, IG state unknown. Every target in the plan is provisional until the first 30 days of data exist.
5. **Site CTAs are dead ends.** "Get the Notebook" has nowhere to go until the Amazon listing exists.
6. **Photography is synthetic.** For a brand about honesty and slowness, AI lifestyle shots of a product that doesn't exist yet is a reputational risk if noticed. Needs real photos of the author proof copy.
7. **Name clearance.** A quick search found no book, podcast or mark called "Before the Sermon". That is not a trademark search. Needs a proper USPTO check before money goes into the brand.
8. **Website platform.** Manus-hosted; ownership, domain, and editability need confirming.
